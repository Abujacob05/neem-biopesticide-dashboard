# Neem Biopesticide Research Dashboard

**Live Dashboard:** https://abujacob05.github.io/neem-biopesticide-dashboard/neem_research_dashboard.html

## About
Interactive data dashboard visualising findings from a peer-reviewed study on the efficacy of Azadirachta indica (Neem) leaf and fruit powders as natural alternatives to synthetic insecticides in controlling Callosobruchus maculatus (Cowpea Weevil).

## Published Paper
Captain-Esoah, M., Abubakari, Y., et al. (2026). International Journal of Plant & Soil Science. DOI: 10.9734/IJPSS/2026/XXXXX
